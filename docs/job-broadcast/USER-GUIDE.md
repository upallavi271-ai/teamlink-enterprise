# Send a job to a company's contact list: 8 steps

1. **Save the job as a template.** On "Add job", fill the job and press "Save as template". On the right, "Your ready template" appears at once: the job post, the WhatsApp message, the SMS and the email.
2. **Read it and change anything.** Click a tab (Job post, WhatsApp, SMS, Email) and edit. The client's name is hidden for you. "How it looks on" shows Naukri, Indeed, Shine and WhatsApp.
3. **Save the job** (Save & Post). The same text goes with the job. On the job page, press "Ready template and post everywhere" to change it later. "Use this" makes a version the one that goes out.
4. **Post it.** Tick the places (Naukri, Indeed, Shine, our careers page, LinkedIn, WhatsApp contacts) and press **Post to all selected places**. Each place shows what really happened: Posted, Queued, Needs your help, Not connected, or Failed with a Retry button.
5. **Import the company's contacts.** Candidates, Contact lists, **Import contacts**. Upload their CSV or Excel file (or use our template). Check the rows (bad numbers, repeats, people who said STOP), give the list a name and where it came from, and save.
6. **Send the job to the list.** Open the list, press **Send a job to this list**. Choose the job. You see the message with a real person's name, how many will get it and how many are skipped (and why), and how many days it takes. Tick that these people gave permission and write why. Press **Send now** (or choose a time, or "first 50 today").
7. **Watch the results.** The results page shows big numbers: Sent, Delivered, Read, Replied, Interested, Not interested, Opted out, Failed. You can Pause, Resume or Cancel. Replies are listed newest first with a chip: Interested, Not interested, Question, Opt-out.
8. **Act on the replies.** Press **Reply** to answer, **Add as candidate** to put the person on the job (or "Add all interested"). Anyone who wrote STOP is never messaged again.

**Good to know**

* Nothing is sent until you press the button. An Admin must switch "Job broadcasts" ON first (Contact lists, Settings). It starts OFF.
* WhatsApp allows only a few messages a day to new people. A list of 5,000 takes about a month. Do not try to go faster: the number can be banned.
* A Recruiter cannot import or send. A BDE sees the numbers hidden. A Manager can look but not send.
* A deleted list can be brought back for 7 days.
